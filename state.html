THE REPUBLIC / STATE STRUCTURE

The State

The constitutional organisation, political system and federal structure of the Federal Republic of Teutonia.

01 — CONSTITUTIONAL ORDER

The foundations of the Republic

Teutonia is a democratic and constitutional federal republic. Public authority derives from the people and is exercised through institutions and procedures established by the Constitution.

01

Democratic Government

The people are the source of public authority. National elections and representative institutions provide the foundation of democratic government.

02

Federal Republic

Federal institutions and constituent states exercise public authority within their constitutionally assigned areas of competence.

03

Separation of Powers

Legislative, executive and judicial authority are distributed among distinct constitutional institutions.

04

Rule of Law

Every public authority must act within the limits established by the Constitution and the law.

The constitutional provisions of Teutonia take precedence over ordinary legislation.
